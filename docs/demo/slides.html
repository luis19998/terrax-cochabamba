Decision support for small farmers: what to sow, when, and with how much water,
from 45 years of NASA data. In Spanish and Quechua.

Live demo: yatirix-cochabamba.netlify.app · Code: github.com/luis19998/yatirix-cochabamba

The problem

  • Cochabamba's valleys get about 480 mm of rain a year, while crops and soil lose more than three times that to evaporation.
  • Rains arrive more irregularly: one frost or one overflowing canal can wipe out a year's harvest.
  • Sowing is decided by habit; repeating the same crop drains the soil and multiplies pests.
  • Peri-urban families (District 9, District 13, La Maica) lack tools in their language.

Who is affected: farming families, irrigation associations and the municipality.

How it works

1. Mark your plotGPS, one tap, corner by corner or traced with a finger. Several plots, each with its colour.
2. Tell what you sowedSeveral crops per season, or "Other": AI estimates its agronomic values.
3. Yatiri X analysesClimate, frost, water balance (FAO-56), slope, soil and municipal zones.
4. Clear adviceBest 3-year rotation, sowing week, irrigation, risks, pests, PDF report.

Three reading levels (simple, medium, advanced) · Spanish and Quechua

NASA data at the core

NASA POWER 1981–2025Daily climate → frost odds, rain, FAO-56 evapotranspiration
NASA SRTMElevation and slope → temperature by altitude, erosion, 3D relief
SMAP · GPM IMERG · MODISSoil moisture, rainfall and greenness layers (NASA GIBS)
+ Local and partner dataCochabamba municipality (GAMC) layers, NOAA GFS 31-member ensemble, SoilGrids, FAO

The numbers come from data and formulas, never from an AI model.

What farmers get

  • 147 crops evaluated for each plot: suitable, with care, or not advised, with the reason.
  • Probabilistic forecast: "6 in 10 scenarios bring frost tonight" for days, weeks and months.
  • Risks and actions: frost, drought, waterlogging, erosion — what to do and what not to do.
  • 64 pests and diseases, 16 invasive species, 12 home remedies, with real photos.
  • Tiri, an AI assistant that answers in Spanish or Quechua using only the plot's data.
  • Plot comparison, 3D terrain, accounts, automatic alerts (n8n: email; WhatsApp ready).

Impact

Example computed live for District 9 (rain-fed potato every year):

54 → 81  rotation score out of 100 when rotating instead of repeating potato

≈ 2,900 m³  of irrigation water saved per hectare in three years with the water-saving rotation

  • Fewer frost losses by sowing outside cold weeks; less fertiliser by rotating with legumes.
  • Built for the municipality: works on the official GAMC layers of every district.
